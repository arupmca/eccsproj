How it is calculated. EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n / ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the loan amount, r is the monthly rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100) and n is the number of EMIs. The EMI is rounded to the nearest rupee. Each month’s interest is charged on the outstanding balance, and the final EMI is adjusted so the balance closes at exactly zero. Figures are indicative; the Secretary’s sanctioned amount and schedule are final.
